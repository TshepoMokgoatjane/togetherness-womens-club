About Us

The Togetherness Women's Club is a community-driven burial society that provides financial support to members and their families during times of bereavement. Through monthly contributions, we ensure that every member has access to dignified burial assistance when they need it most. Our system tracks membership, dependents, contributions, and claims to ensure transparency and accountability.
