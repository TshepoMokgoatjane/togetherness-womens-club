About Us

Our story, our mission, our values

Our Story

Togetherness Women's Club was founded on the belief that women are stronger together. What started as a small group of women supporting each other has grown into a vibrant community united by compassion, solidarity, and shared purpose.

Our members come from all walks of life, bound by a common commitment to uplift one another through monthly contributions that provide a safety net during life's most challenging moments.

Our Mission

To empower women through collective financial support, providing dignity and care to our members and their families during times of bereavement, while fostering a community of sisterhood and mutual upliftment.

Our Vision

A community where every woman feels supported, valued, and empowered — knowing that in her time of need, her sisters will stand beside her with unwavering solidarity.
